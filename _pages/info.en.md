## About Supernihil

Supernihil is an archive of audiovisual experiments, interactive installations and digital fragments. Through an investigation into the materiality of technology, the archive explores the tension between human intention and the unpredictability of the machine.

- **Data transformation:** translating binary code into sensory experience – from bytes to sound, and from sound to image.
- **Aesthetics of failure:** glitch, noise and the visual artifacts that emerge when systems are pushed to their limits.
- **Digital ghosts:** time-shifting, feedback loops and the repurposing of obsolete hardware.

Based in Svendborg, Denmark. Often performs live under *Sekvens* or as part of the radio and TV network *BURO*.
