The silos on Østre Kaj have been part of Svendborg's skyline for decades and are scheduled for demolition. The site collects photos, videos, sound recordings, stories and objects into a shared public archive. Contributions are shown immediately and can be hidden afterwards by moderation. Static frontend on GitHub Pages; uploads via a Cloudflare Worker with D1 database and R2 storage, Turnstile spam protection and a fixed monthly cost ceiling. Danish and English.
