body_en: |
  Each concerto lasts exactly as long as its meteorological season: spring 1 March–31 May, summer 1 June–31 August, autumn 1 September–30 November, winter 1 December–end of February. The playhead is always at the present moment. The recording is time-stretched in the browser with Paulstretch (Nasca Octavian Paul), compiled to WebAssembly, by a factor of about 12,000; one second of music lasts about 3½ hours. Only the audio segments around the playhead are downloaded. The printed score shows the current position.

  Recording: John Harrison (violin), Robert Turizziani (conductor), The Wichita State University Chamber Players. CC BY-SA 4.0, via the Internet Archive.

Vivaldi's The Four Seasons stretched across a full calendar year.

<!--more-->

Hver koncert varer præcis lige så længe som sin meteorologiske årstid: forår 1. marts–31. maj, sommer 1. juni–31. august, efterår 1. september–30. november, vinter 1. december–udgangen af februar. Afspilningspositionen er altid det aktuelle øjeblik. Optagelsen tidsstrækkes i browseren med Paulstretch (Nasca Octavian Paul), kompileret til WebAssembly, med en faktor på ca. 12.000; ét sekund musik varer ca. 3½ time. Kun lydsegmenterne omkring afspilningspositionen hentes. Det trykte partitur viser den aktuelle position.

Optagelse: John Harrison (violin), Robert Turizziani (dirigent), The Wichita State University Chamber Players. CC BY-SA 4.0, via Internet Archive.
