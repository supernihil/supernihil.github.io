Web shop and commercial in the style of a 1990s TV shopping channel for a chocolate bar sold as a gift for people you can't stand. Slogans: "A little chocolate. A lot of attitude." and "Because 'thank you' is overrated." The shop takes pre-orders, sells print-on-demand merchandise and can include a printed note with each order. The commercial is published on YouTube.
